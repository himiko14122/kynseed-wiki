## Thornborough's Founding Legends and Mythological Roots

The founding of Thornborough is steeped in Kynseed mythology that predates the town's first wooden walls. According to community reports and the stories villagers tell around the tavern hearth, the settlement grew around a single sacred oak that stood where the town square now hosts the seasonal festival grounds. That oak wasn't ordinary—it was said to be a direct descendant of the original tree that produced the first Kynseed, the magical acorn that grants mortals the ability to pass their consciousness down through their bloodline.

The **Whispering Oak**, as older NPCs call it, was supposedly planted by a figure known only as the **First Keeper**, a mysterious wanderer who arrived in the Vale with nothing but a satchel of acorns and a promise to protect the land from the darkness that lurks beyond the borders. While the tree itself is long gone, its stump remains visible near the town center, and players have reported that interacting with it triggers unique dialogue from certain villagers who remember the old stories passed down through their families.

The Kynseed legends surrounding Thornborough's founding also explain the town's unusual architecture. The blacksmith's forge sits on the exact spot where the First Keeper supposedly defeated a **Glimmerfang**, a shadowy wolf-like creature from the Fae realm, using nothing but a hammer and the light of the acorn. That battle supposedly created the crater that later became the town's central well, and the forge was built there to "keep the fire burning against the dark," as the elderly blacksmith often reminds newcomers.

According to Kynseed lore shared by long-time players, the Fae's bargain with Thornborough's founders created a delicate balance that still affects gameplay.
